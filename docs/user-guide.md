# Blueprint Support — User Guide

## What Is Blueprint Support?

Blueprint Support is an AI-powered chat widget that embeds on your website and handles visitor conversations in real time. It captures leads, books appointments, and responds to questions — all without any manual effort from your team.

## Who Is It For?

- Local service businesses that want to respond to website visitors immediately, even outside of business hours
- Businesses that lose potential customers because no one is available to answer questions on their site
- Teams looking to automate initial contact with new prospects before handing off to a salesperson or support rep

## Key Concepts

- **Widget** — the chat bubble that appears on your website. Visitors click it to start a conversation with your AI.
- **Tenant** — your business's workspace within Blueprint Support. Your configuration, conversations, and leads are all tied to your tenant.
- **Conversation** — a single back-and-forth exchange between a visitor and the AI, from first message to last.
- **Lead** — a website visitor whose contact information has been captured during a conversation.
- **Instance** — a specific deployment of the chat widget. If you run multiple websites or brands, each can have its own instance.
- **Booking** — an appointment created directly from the chat widget when a visitor schedules time with your business.

## Getting Started

1. Log in to your Blueprint portal using the credentials you received by email.
2. Navigate to Blueprint Support in your portal.
3. Open Settings and enter your business name, description, and support hours.
4. Write a greeting message — the first text a visitor sees when they open the chat.
5. Connect your calendar in the Booking Settings section to enable appointment booking.
6. Set up lead capture by confirming which contact fields you want to collect.
7. Copy the widget embed code from the Widget section.
8. Paste the code into your website and test it by opening your site and starting a conversation.

## Core Features

### Embeddable Chat Widget

The chat widget installs on your website with a single snippet of code and works on any page you place it.

- Visitors click the chat bubble to start a conversation instantly
- The AI responds in real time — no waiting, no hold music
- The widget adapts to mobile screens as well as desktop browsers
- You can match the widget color to your brand

## Step-by-Step Walkthroughs

### Installing the Widget on Your Website

1. Log in to your Blueprint portal and open Blueprint Support.
2. Go to Widget Settings and confirm your business name and greeting message are set.
3. Click Copy Embed Code to copy the installation snippet.
4. Open your website editing tools or send the code to your web developer.
5. Paste the code just before the closing body tag on every page where you want the widget to appear.
6. Save and publish your website.
7. Open your website in a new browser window and look for the chat bubble in the corner.
8. Click the bubble and send a test message to confirm the AI responds.

Outcome: Your chat widget is live and ready to engage visitors.

### Capturing a Lead and Following Up

1. A visitor comes to your website, opens the chat, and asks a question.
2. The AI responds and collects the visitor's name and email during the conversation.
3. You receive a notification alert — check your email or SMS depending on your notification settings.
4. Log in to Blueprint Support and go to your Leads section.
5. Click the new lead to see the full conversation and their contact details.
6. Reach out to the visitor directly using their email or phone number.

Outcome: You have a warm lead and a full record of what they were interested in.

### Setting Up Appointment Booking

1. Go to Settings in your Blueprint Support dashboard.
2. Click Booking Settings and choose your calendar provider — Google Calendar, Cal.com, or Calendly.
3. Follow the on-screen steps to connect your calendar account.
4. Set your available hours — the days and times you want the AI to offer for booking.
5. Save your settings.
6. Open your website and start a test conversation. Ask the AI to book an appointment.
7. Confirm that available time slots appear and that you can complete a test booking.

Outcome: Visitors can book appointments from your website at any time, with no manual scheduling on your part.

### Reviewing Conversation Quality

1. In your Blueprint Support dashboard, go to Conversations.
2. Sort by date to find the most recent exchanges.
3. Click through several conversations and read the transcripts.
4. Note any questions the AI answered incorrectly or awkwardly.
5. If you find gaps, contact your Blueprint partner to update the AI's configuration or knowledge.

Outcome: You have a clear picture of how the AI is performing and what needs improvement.

## Settings & Configuration

**Business Name** — The name your AI agent uses when introducing itself to visitors. Use your real business name.

**Greeting Message** — The first message visitors see when they open the chat bubble. Keep it short and specific to what you do. "Hi! Ask me anything about our cleaning services." works better than a generic hello.

**Support Hours** — The hours your business operates. The AI references these when visitors ask about availability or response times.

**Widget Color** — The color of the chat bubble. Pick something visible against your website's background.

**Lead Capture Fields** — Which contact details the AI collects. Name and email are standard. Add phone number only if you need it.

**Booking Calendar** — The calendar the AI uses to show available appointment slots. Connect the one your business already uses.

**Notification Settings** — Where alerts go when new leads or bookings come in. Set this to a channel you check frequently.

**Widget Position** — Whether the chat bubble appears in the bottom-right or bottom-left corner of your website.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Write your greeting message to match what visitors are most likely to need. If most visitors ask about pricing, mention pricing in the greeting.
- Check your Conversations log at least once a week to see what questions are coming in. This tells you what content to add to your site.
- Enable SMS notifications for new leads so you can follow up quickly — speed matters when a prospect is still on your site.
- Test the widget from a phone to confirm it looks and works well on mobile. Most website visitors use mobile devices.
- Set realistic booking availability — do not show every slot if you cannot actually take every appointment.
- If you run multiple brands or locations, use separate instances rather than trying to configure one widget to handle everything.
- Keep your business description in Settings accurate. The AI uses it to answer questions about what you do.
- Review captured leads within 24 hours while the conversation is still fresh in the visitor's mind.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** The chat widget is not appearing on my website.
**Solution:** Confirm the embed code was pasted before the closing body tag, not in the head section. Also check that your website was saved and published after the code was added. Clear your browser cache and reload the page.

**Problem:** The AI is not responding to visitor messages.
**Solution:** Check that your Blueprint Support account is active and that the widget instance is enabled. If the issue continues, contact your Blueprint partner — there may be a service interruption.

**Problem:** Leads are not showing up in my dashboard.
**Solution:** Confirm the lead capture feature is enabled in your Settings. Check that the AI is asking for contact information during conversations. If the widget is working but leads are missing, contact your Blueprint partner.

**Problem:** The booking calendar is not showing available slots.
**Solution:** Check that your calendar is still connected in Booking Settings. Reconnect the calendar if the status shows disconnected. Confirm that your availability hours are set correctly and that your calendar has open slots in those windows.

**Problem:** I am not receiving notification alerts for new leads.
**Solution:** Go to Notification Settings and confirm your email or phone number is correct. For email, check your spam folder. For SMS, confirm the number includes the correct country code.

**Problem:** The widget looks broken or misaligned on my website.
**Solution:** Some website themes conflict with embedded scripts. Share this information with your Blueprint partner — they can provide a version of the embed code adjusted for your specific site setup.

**Problem:** A visitor said they submitted their information but I cannot find their lead.
**Solution:** Search for the visitor by the date or partial name in your Leads section. If it still does not appear, the conversation may not have reached the lead capture stage. Ask your Blueprint partner to check the conversation log on the backend.

## FAQs

**Does Blueprint Support work with my existing website?**
Yes. As long as your website allows you to add a small code snippet — which almost all website platforms do — Blueprint Support will work. It is compatible with WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, and custom-built sites.

**Can I control what the AI talks about?**
Yes. The AI's responses are based on the business description and any content your Blueprint partner configures for you. It stays focused on topics relevant to your business.

**What happens when the AI cannot answer a question?**
The AI tells the visitor it is not able to help with that specific question and offers to pass along their contact information to your team for follow-up.

**Is there a limit to how many conversations the AI can handle?**
No. The AI handles unlimited simultaneous conversations. Every visitor gets an immediate response regardless of how many people are on your site at the same time.

**Can I see what visitors are saying in real time?**
You can view all conversations in your dashboard after they happen. Real-time monitoring is not a standard view, but all transcripts are available immediately after each conversation ends.

**How quickly does the AI respond to visitors?**
Responses typically appear within 1-2 seconds. Visitors experience it as a natural, real-time conversation.

**Can I use Blueprint Support on more than one website?**
Yes. You can create multiple instances, each with its own configuration and embed code. Manage them all from the same dashboard.

**What information does the AI collect from visitors?**
By default, the AI collects name, email, and optionally phone number when capturing a lead. It does not collect any payment or sensitive personal information.

**Can visitors book appointments on weekends?**
Yes, if you have set weekend availability in your Booking Settings. The AI only shows the time slots you have configured as available.

**How do I remove the widget from my website?**
Delete the embed code snippet from your website's HTML and save the page. The widget will stop appearing immediately.
